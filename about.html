Our Story

From a bold idea to a trusted modular partner

Evaregi was founded on a simple conviction: that quality buildings shouldn't take months to deliver or cost more than they should. By bringing manufacturing precision to construction, we help our clients occupy safe, comfortable and durable spaces in a fraction of the usual time.

Today we serve mining houses, contractors, government bodies, schools and commercial clients — handling everything from ground preparation and civil works to electrical, plumbing and final fit-out. Our modular units are engineered to perform in the region's toughest and most remote environments.

As a registered and fully compliant contractor, we are equipped to deliver both private commissions and public-sector tenders with complete accountability.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, cost-effective modular and prefabricated buildings that empower industries to operate and grow — safely, comfortably and without delay.
