Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much faster is modular construction?

Because units are manufactured off-site at the same time as ground preparation, most projects are delivered 30–50% faster than equivalent conventional builds.

Can modular buildings be used permanently?

Yes. Our steel-framed structures are engineered as durable, long-life buildings. They can serve as permanent facilities or be relocated when your needs change.

Do you handle site works and foundations too?

Absolutely. We offer fully turnkey delivery — clearing, levelling, foundations, civil works, electrical, plumbing and fit-out — all under one contract.

Can I rent instead of buying?

Yes. We offer flexible rental for temporary requirements as well as outright purchase for permanent needs — whichever suits your project and budget.

Do you work on remote mining sites?

Our buildings are purpose-built for remote and harsh environments. We regularly deliver offices, camps and ablution facilities to off-grid mining locations.

How do I get a quote?

Simply use our contact form, call us, or message us on WhatsApp with your requirements. We typically respond with a proposal within two working days.
